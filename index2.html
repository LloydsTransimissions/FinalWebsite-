Who We Are

A Family Built On Trust

Lloyd's Transmissions & Drivetrain is a father-and-son shop right here in Tucson. We built this business on the belief that every customer deserves straight answers, honest diagnostics, and work done right the first time.

From daily drivers to serious off-road rigs, we've seen it all. We specialize in the complex stuff: transmissions, differentials, transfer cases, and full drivetrain systems. We take the time to explain what's wrong and why before we ever touch a bolt.

No upselling. No guesswork. Just solid work at fair prices, the way a family shop should operate. It's why 98% of our new customers find us through word of mouth.

What We Do

Our Services

From transmission rebuilds to full drivetrain overhauls, we handle the jobs that keep your vehicle moving, on road or off.

01

Transmission Repair & Rebuild

Manual transmission diagnostics, repair, and full rebuilds. We restore shifting performance and extend the life of your drivetrain at a fraction of dealership cost.

Auto & Manual
02

Differential Service

Front, rear, and limited-slip differential rebuilds and repairs, plus E-locker installs and differential housing upgrades. Critical for off-road performance, towing, and proper power distribution to all four wheels on any terrain.

Front & Rear
03

Transfer Case

Transfer case inspection, repair, and rebuild for 4WD and AWD vehicles. Keep your 4-wheel drive system switching smoothly whether you're on pavement or deep on the trail.

4WD / AWD
04

Drivetrain & Driveshaft

Full drivetrain inspections, U-joint replacement, and CV axle service. If it transfers power from your engine to your wheels, we handle it.

Complete Drivetrain
05

Off-Road Builds & Upgrades

Upgrading your rig for the trails? We work on off-road vehicles and handle drivetrain upgrades, gear ratio changes, and heavy-duty component installs for serious wheelers.

Trail Ready
06

General Auto Repair

Beyond drivetrain, we handle general automotive maintenance and repairs. One shop, one relationship. We're here for the long haul whether it's a fluid change or a major repair.
